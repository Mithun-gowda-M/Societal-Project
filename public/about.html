Technology in Service of Humanity

Every Drop Connects a Life.

BloodConnect is an advanced healthcare technology platform dedicated to eliminating critical delays in emergency blood access. By uniting predictive AI donor response modeling, real-time hospital stock transparency, and ethical clinical safety protocols, we empower citizens and healthcare providers to save lives in minutes.

🎯

Predictive Response Speed

Traditional donor coordination relies on broadcast panic messaging. Our AI models analyze geofenced proximity, historical confirmation reliability, and cooldown freshness to mobilize the exact donors most likely to arrive within 15 minutes.

🏥

Bangalore Transfusion Network

Directly synchronizes inventory across 10 prominent Bangalore blood centres (BMCRI Victoria, Bowring, NIMHANS, Rotary TTK, Rashtrotthana, Kidwai, Manipal, Narayana Hrudayalaya, Red Cross, and St. John's) so doctors and families never face dead ends.

🛡️

Clinical Safety & 90-Day Cooldown

Donor wellness is paramount. We strictly enforce a 3-month (90-day) recovery period between whole blood donations, Aadhaar ID verification against bots and spam, and explicit medical clearance overrides for emergency triage.

⚕️

Clinical Assessment & Transfusion Disclaimer

BloodConnect provides digital donor coordination, preliminary algorithmic eligibility screening, and hospital inventory tracking. Blood group compatibility alone does not establish clinical suitability for transfusion. Final compatibility, infectious disease screening, cross-matching, and phlebotomy clearance must be verified and performed by qualified healthcare and blood-bank professionals on-site in accordance with the National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) and NACO standards.
